### STXF benchmark naming discrepancy

The discrepancy is retained here rather than silently normalized:

- The current STXF issuer product page identifies **Bloomberg US Large Cap Index** as the benchmark and describes it as a float market-cap-weighted benchmark of 500 of the most highly capitalized U.S. companies. Checked 2026-10-28: [issuer product page](https://www.strivefunds.com/stxf).
- The October 31, 2025 prospectus uses the name **Bloomberg 500 Index**. Checked 2026-10-28: [SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592900/000159290025003298/ck0001592900-20251028.htm).
- The June 30, 2026 shareholder report describes a benchmark change effective **January 26, 2024** from **Solactive GBS United States 500 Index** to **Bloomberg US Large Cap Index**. Its performance discussion also uses the inconsistent label “Bloomberg 500 Index” in a performance row. The report covers the period ending 2026-06-30 and was checked 2026-10-28: [SEC shareholder report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592900/000159290026004198/ck0001592900-20260630.htm).
- The fund ticker changed from **STRV to STXF effective June 8, 2026**. The ticker-change supplement says references to STRV were replaced with STXF; this was a ticker change, not a benchmark change. Checked 2026-10-28: [SEC ticker-change supplement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592900/000159290026002347/strivestrv-stxf497etickerc.htm).

The current issuer page and latest shareholder report support **Bloomberg US Large Cap Index** as the proposed current landing-page label. The issuer has not formally reconciled the differing benchmark names across the prospectus and report. This research does **not** claim that “Bloomberg 500 Index” and “Bloomberg US Large Cap Index” are officially interchangeable. Proposed landing-page wording:

> STXF seeks to track the Bloomberg US Large Cap Index, a float-adjusted, market-cap-weighted index of 500 large U.S. companies. Before January 26, 2024, the fund tracked the Solactive GBS United States 500 Index.

If space is limited, keep “Bloomberg US Large Cap Index” as the current label and link to the source note rather than substituting “Bloomberg 500 Index.”

## Comparison methodology and interpretation

The funds represent different U.S. equity approaches, not interchangeable alternatives:

- **STXF:** broad U.S. large-cap exposure.
- **STXG:** growth-oriented U.S. large- and mid-cap equities.
- **STXV:** value-oriented U.S. large- and mid-cap equities.

For a consistent educational comparison, use the same fields for each fund: company-size/geographic scope, named benchmark and selection methodology, and stated annual fund operating expenses. Explain that “growth” and “value” are index-provider classifications based on the stated metrics, not guarantees about future returns. Do not rank the funds or imply that one strategy is universally better. This is not a personalized investment recommendation.

All three funds are from the same issuer's product range and use the same adviser/sub-adviser structure. That provides a consistent disclosure framework for this small comparison, but it limits the comparison to one provider's fund selection and methodologies; it is not a survey of the broader ETF market.

## Known limitations and unresolved questions

- **STXF benchmark naming:** Current issuer materials support Bloomberg US Large Cap Index as the current label, but the 2025 prospectus and part of the 2026 shareholder report use Bloomberg 500 Index. The issuer has not formally reconciled the names. See the discrepancy section above.
- **Czech investor access:** Czech retail investor availability and broker-specific access were not verified. A U.S. exchange listing does not establish that a particular Czech investor can trade a fund.
- **PRIIPs KID:** No PRIIPs Key Information Document was located in the reviewed U.S. issuer materials. This is not proof that no such document exists. The EU PRIIPs Regulation establishes the KID framework, including preparation and provision duties; CNB guidance summarizes the general KID requirement, and the CNB Q&A explains practical topics such as language, publication/provision, and execution-only services. These sources do **not** establish broker-specific availability of STXF, STXG, or STXV to Czech retail investors. Fund-level KID availability and broker access remain unverified.
- **Changing data:** Fund names, listings, benchmarks, fees, and other financial information may change. Recheck current issuer materials and filings immediately before publication.
- **Comparison scope:** The funds share broad U.S. equity exposure but use different benchmark universes and style classifications. The comparison should explain those differences rather than imply direct equivalence.
